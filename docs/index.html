About this Project — Size 4.5 Focus

This site exists because a 14K or 18K stamp is not proof. Our goal is to identify sellers who are transparent about metal weight, allowing you to calculate the true value of what you are buying.

Current focus: Ring size 4.5 — one of the smallest standard sizes (US 4.5 = ~48mm circumference, ~15.3mm diameter). Many sellers do not offer this size, and even fewer publish the gram weight for it. We verified over 20 entries from 10+ sellers specifically for size 4.5 availability.

Gold ring — online buying checklist

  1. Solid gold: Listing must explicitly say solid gold.
  2. Weight: Get the ring’s weight in grams. Legitimate sellers (see table) publish this.
  3. Price sanity check: Compare price to (grams × karat-fraction × spot price).
  4. Payment: Use credit card for chargeback protection.
  5. Independent verification: After arrival, take it to a jeweler for XRF testing.

Findings: Size 4.5 Ring Market — Transparency Gap & Verified Options

Our search for solid gold rings in size 4.5 (one of the smallest adult sizes, ~48mm circumference) revealed a particularly acute transparency gap. Most major retailers (Blue Nile, James Allen, Tiffany) do not publish gram weights for any size, and many smaller sellers start at size 5 or 6, excluding size 4.5 entirely.

Sellers who DO publish weight and offer size 4.5 (see evidence table for prices and source links):

  • Menē (24K) — Offers size 4.5 in Narrow Classic, Narrow Flat, Classic, Flat, and Wide Classic bands. Sold by weight; price fluctuates with spot gold.
  • Quince (14K) — Publishes weight for dome bands. Multiple other 14K styles available in size 4.5 but without weight.
  • Avariah Diamonds (14K) — Amazon seller that explicitly publishes weight for size 4.5.
  • 7879 (24K) — Stacker Ring with published weight, available in US size 4 (close to 4.5).
  • JewelryWeb (14K) — Walmart listing includes weight in grams in the product title for size 4.5.

IRREGULARITY FLAGGED: Quince lists a single weight for their dome band products without specifying which ring size that weight corresponds to. Buyers should verify that the published weight matches their ordered size. Similarly, Oradina lists weight per size for some rings but their After Hours ring page was inaccessible during verification.
