Findings: Ring Size 4.5 Market & Transparency Analysis

Investigation of small-size ring availability, weight disclosures, and retail pricing patterns.

Our investigation into solid gold rings in US size 4.5 (~48mm circumference, ~15.3mm diameter) revealed that small ring sizes represent an acute transparency test. Many mass-market retailers start sizing at 5 or 6, while others offer size 4.5 only as custom non-returnable orders without publishing gram weights.

Top High-Transparency Brands

  • Automic Gold: Publishes approx gram weight on every ring page, offers sizes 2–16 in quarter increments.
  • Costco: Publishes exact weight charts per ring size range (e.g. 4.56g for 18K 4mm band).
  • Menē: Pure 24K investment bands with exact gram weights and transparent spot-linked pricing.
  • Ritani: Publishes metal weight on settings and supports full quarter sizing (3–9 range).
  • 7879: Full bullion transparency with itemized metal value and manufacturing cost.

Flagged Irregularities & Exclusions

  • Macy’s 4mm Band (Excluded): Customer review verified stamp reads '14k fg' (gold-filled), contradicting solid 14K title.
  • Quince Low Dome Single Weight: Publishes one weight for 3mm bands without distinguishing size variants.
  • Zales Price Drift: Live schema pricing differed from cache snapshots; live page recorded.
  • Catbird Mignon Stock: Size 4.5 variant marked out of stock; noted in catalog for completeness.

Solid Gold Buyer’s Checklist

Essential 5-step checklist to protect yourself when purchasing solid gold jewelry online.

  1. 1
    Demand Explicit 'Solid Gold' Language

    Reject ambiguous terms like 'gold finish', 'gold vermeil' (plated sterling silver), 'gold-filled' (brass core with thin gold shell), or 'bonded gold'. FTC guidelines strictly define solid gold.

  2. 2
    Verify Metal Weight in Grams Before Ordering

    Never purchase without knowing the gram weight. Legitimate sellers (see High Transparency filter in the table) publish exact or approximate gram weights for each ring size.

  3. 3
    Perform a Melt-Value Sanity Check

    Multiply: Total Weight (g) × Karat Purity × Spot Price per Gram. Any listing priced below raw melt value is almost certainly fraudulent or hollow.

  4. 4
    Pay Exclusively with Credit Card

    Ensure credit card chargeback protection under Fair Credit Billing laws in case independent testing discovers lower gold purity than advertised.

  5. 5
    Independent Post-Delivery Verification

    Immediately test with a 0.01g digital scale, inspect hallmarks under 10× loupe, and obtain non-destructive XRF spectrometry testing at an independent jeweler.

Independent Post-Delivery Testing Guide

How to verify metal authenticity, karat purity, and weight once your ring arrives.

1. Precision Scale Test

Weigh the ring on a calibrated 0.01g digital jewelry scale. Compare directly against the seller's published weight (should be within ±5% tolerance).

2. Hallmark & Maker's Mark

Examine the inner band under a 10× loupe. Look for legal millesimal fineness stamps: 585 (14K), 750 (18K), 999 (24K), or 950 (Platinum).

3. Specific Gravity Test

Determine density using Archimedes' water displacement method. 14K yellow gold has density ~13.0–13.5 g/cm³; 18K is ~15.2–15.6 g/cm³; 24K is 19.3 g/cm³.

4. XRF Spectrometry

Visit a qualified independent jeweler with an X-ray fluorescence (XRF) analyzer. Provides non-destructive elemental breakdown in under 30 seconds.
