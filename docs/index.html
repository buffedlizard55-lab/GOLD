Gold ring — online buying checklist

  1. Solid gold: Listing must explicitly say solid gold.
  2. Weight: Get the ring’s weight in grams. Legitimate sellers (see table) publish this.
  3. Price sanity check: Compare price to (grams × karat-fraction × spot price).
  4. Payment: Use credit card for chargeback protection.
  5. Independent verification: After arrival, take it to a jeweler for XRF testing.
